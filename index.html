HyperVision: Designing In-Situ Interaction for Generative Re-Rendering in Mixed Reality

Suibi Che-Chuan Weng · Anika Mahajan · Fatemeh Hadavandkhani · Pritalee Kadam · Yan-Ming Chiou · Amy Banić · Ryo Suzuki · Ellen Yi-Luen Do

HyperVision enables users to generatively transform their mixed-reality passthrough view using text, sketches, masks, and virtual elements. A 20-participant study examines how transformation strength affects recognizability, creativity, coherence, and control.

Mixed Reality Generative AI Human–AI Interaction User Study
Submitted · CHI 2027

DreamPortal: Generative In-Situ Behavior Authoring in Cyber-Physical Environments through Mixed Reality

Suibi Che-Chuan Weng · Fatemeh Hadavandkhani · Ryo Suzuki · Ellen Yi-Luen Do

DreamPortal allows users to author behaviors across virtual objects, physical environments, and IoT devices using natural language in mixed reality. A 20-participant study examines how people express, execute, inspect, revise, and compose behaviors.

Human–AI Collaboration Mixed Reality IoT Research through Design

About Naz

Saharnaz (Naz) Hadavandkhani is an HCI researcher, UI/UX designer, and creative technologist at the University of Colorado Boulder.

Her background spans mechanical engineering, art research, and creative technology, informing her work across interactive systems, human–AI interaction, mixed reality, prototyping, and design.

Since 2024, Naz has worked in CU Boulder’s Digital Fabrication Lab, supporting students with prototyping and fabrication using 3D printers, laser cutters, and other digital fabrication tools.
